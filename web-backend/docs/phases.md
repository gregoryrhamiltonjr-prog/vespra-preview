## Explicit non-claims

No reservation was made, no identity or age was verified by a provider, no payment or subscription was created, no push was sent, no email was actually delivered in this environment, no public hosting was provisioned, no moderators were hired, no domain or trademark was cleared, and no native binary was signed or published.

The production-mode guard rejects unsupported modes. Private-account functionality is a testable software increment, not permission to invite real members. Disabled integrations display an honest unavailable/manual state; a generic adapter contract is not a claim of compatibility with a specific partner.
