Evaluation terms

Adults 18 and older only. This is a private software evaluation, not an approved public dating service. Do not enter real sensitive information. Treat other participants with dignity; harassment, discrimination, impersonation, fraud, threats, coercion, and sexual content involving minors are prohibited. You can decline any conversation or date at any time.

No reservation, identity check, subscription, payment, or notification delivery is guaranteed. A shared plan is not a restaurant booking. Any booking made outside VESPRA must be changed or cancelled with that provider. No public-member launch is authorized by these evaluation terms.

Reports enter an operator queue. Human review depends on an assigned, staffed operator. There is no guaranteed response time. Restrictions may be appealed; a different reviewer is required for an appeal. This is not an emergency service. In immediate danger in the US, call 911.

Evaluation privacy notice

Stored information: first name, calculated age, optional bio and interests, approximate neighborhood, email, password hash, session hashes, explicit consent version, directed interest, matches, messages, date plans, private reflections, blocks, reports, appeals, and limited operational audit events. Submitted birth date is used to calculate age and is not retained. Email is for account verification and recovery, not public profile display. No identity documents, precise GPS, employer names, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking are collected.

Profiles start paused. Email confirmation is required before opting into discovery and does not verify identity or age. Company privacy is the default. Only matches can chat. Private reflections are returned only to their author. Moderator access is restricted and queue reads/decisions are audited.

The active database is local SQLite. Its data is not encrypted at rest by the app; the operator must provide encrypted storage and appropriate access control. SMTP, if configured, transmits account verification/recovery email through the operator’s email provider. No reservation, billing, identity or push provider is active by default.

Self-service export requires password confirmation. Account deletion immediately removes the active profile, credentials, sessions, shared local conversations and plans, associated reports and reflections; this also removes those local shared records from the other participant. It does not erase downloaded exports, outside bookings, or operator backups. Logical deletion is not forensic disk erasure. No legal-hold or evidence-preservation workflow is implemented for this evaluation.

Operator-invoked retention clears expired sessions/challenges/rate-limit entries, notifications older than 30 days, mail older than two days, and audit events older than 90 days. This job does not run unless invoked. Profiles, messages, plans and reports otherwise persist until account deletion or explicit operator action. Backup retention is an operator obligation and is not automatically enforced by this app.

Before any real-member use

These are evaluation notices, not final legal terms. A real deployment needs the legal entity, jurisdiction-specific legal review, privacy contact, sensitive-data consent analysis, age-assurance decision, audited hosting, retention and preservation rules, staffed safety operations, and finalized rights/appeals procedures.
